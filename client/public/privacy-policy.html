Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Umoja respects your privacy. This notice explains how personal information is handled when you visit this promotional website, contact us, or continue to the Umoja customer portal.

Information we may collect

  • Information you choose to provide when contacting Umoja or applying through the customer portal.
  • Basic technical and usage information, such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, and approximate location, when you consent to optional analytics.
  • Referral or reseller identifiers included in a link used to visit the website.

How we use information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, process applications, provide and improve Umoja services, understand website performance, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Analytics and storage

Optional analytics only loads after you select “Accept analytics.” Your preference is saved in your browser so the website can remember your choice. You can reopen Cookie settings from the website footer at any time.

Sharing and retention

Umoja only shares personal information with authorised service providers when needed to operate its services, or where required by law. Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you, or object to certain processing.

Security and external services

Umoja uses reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. Links to the customer portal, WhatsApp, and other external services are governed by those services’ own privacy and security practices.
